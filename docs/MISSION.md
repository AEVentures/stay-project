# Mission

Tyler's Light exists to make suicide-prevention information free, findable,
and usable by anyone — dedicated to the memory of Tyler McNeil.

## The problem

Prevention information exists, but it is scattered across institutions,
buried in PDFs, or gated behind logins. People in crisis, and the people who
love them, often do not find the right resource in the moment they need it.

## What we build

1. **A verified crisis-resource directory** — hotlines, text lines, and
   warmlines, by country and language, kept accurate over time.
2. **Plain-language education** — warning signs, protective factors, and
   how to have a direct, caring conversation with someone who is struggling.
3. **Reusable, embeddable tools** — a "Get Help" widget and structured data
   any school, employer, or community site can use for free.
4. **Safe messaging by default** — everything here follows WHO and AFSP
   safe-messaging guidelines. We do not describe methods, do not
   sensationalize loss, and always pair difficult content with a path to
   help.

## What we will not do

- We will not describe or discuss methods of suicide.
- We will not present suicide as a solution, an escape, or something
  glamorous, mysterious, or simplistic.
- We will not collect personal health information or require anyone to
  identify themselves to get help.
- We will not run ads or sell user data.

## Why "Tyler's Light"

This project is dedicated to Tyler McNeil. It exists because he mattered,
and because the right information at the right moment can be the difference
between someone reaching out and someone going without help. See
[DEDICATION.md](./DEDICATION.md).
