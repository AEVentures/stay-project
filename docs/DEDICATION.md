# In memory of Tyler McNeil

Tyler's Light is dedicated to Tyler McNeil.

This project was started because Tyler mattered — and because suicide takes
people who are loved, missed, and irreplaceable. We built this in his memory
and in the hope that free, accessible resources reach someone, somewhere, in
time.

## This page is intentionally left open

We do not want to write Tyler's story for his family and friends. If you
knew Tyler and would like to add:

- A short biography or tribute
- A photo
- Words from family or friends
- A cause, hobby, or memory fund in his name

please contribute it directly:

- Open a pull request editing this file, following the format below, or
- [Open an issue](https://github.com/AEVentures/tylers-light/issues/new)
  and a maintainer will add it with care, or
- Email the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for contact) if
  you would prefer not to use GitHub.

We will never publish personal details about Tyler without explicit
permission from his family.

## Suggested format

```markdown
## Tyler McNeil

[Years] — [one or two sentences about who he was]

[A short story, memory, or tribute — 2-4 paragraphs]

[Optional: a cause or organization to support in his memory]
```

## Editorial guidelines for this page

Per suicide-prevention safe-messaging guidance, tributes on this page should:

- Focus on Tyler's life, personality, and the people who loved him — not the
  circumstances or method of his death.
- Avoid language that suggests suicide was inevitable, simple, or a solution.
- Include a path to help (see the [Resources](../README.md#resources)
  section) near any personal or emotional content.
