# Safe Messaging Guidelines for Contributors

Tyler's Light follows established public-health guidance on how to talk
about suicide safely and effectively. Every contributor — writing copy,
reviewing a PR, or adding a resource — should read this first.

Primary sources this project follows:

- World Health Organization, *Preventing Suicide: A Resource for Media
  Professionals* (adapted here for a general-public website).
- American Foundation for Suicide Prevention (AFSP) / Reporting on Suicide,
  [reportingonsuicide.org](https://reportingonsuicide.org).
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline messaging guidance.

## Do

- Use factual, matter-of-fact language: "died by suicide," not "committed
  suicide" (a term with criminal/moral connotations) or "successful
  suicide."
- Emphasize that suicide is preventable and that help works.
- Include a crisis resource (988, Crisis Text Line, etc.) near any content
  discussing suicide, grief, or personal stories.
- Focus on warning signs, protective factors, and how to help.
- Use content warnings where appropriate.

## Do not

- Do not describe or depict method, location, or other specifics of a
  suicide or suicide attempt.
- Do not use dramatic headlines, images, or language ("epidemic,"
  "skyrocketing") that can be sensationalizing.
- Do not present suicide as inexplicable, simplistic, or a solution to
  problems.
- Do not imply that a single event (a breakup, a failure, a loss) "caused"
  a suicide — most deaths by suicide involve multiple, complex factors,
  often including an underlying mental health condition.
- Do not publish personal details about people who have died by suicide
  without explicit family/guardian consent (see
  [DEDICATION.md](./DEDICATION.md) for how this applies to Tyler McNeil's
  page specifically).

## Pull request checklist

Any PR that adds or edits user-facing copy should confirm:

- [ ] No method, location, or means of suicide is described.
- [ ] A crisis resource is present near any sensitive content.
- [ ] Language matches the "do" list above.
- [ ] Statistics are sourced (see [RESOURCES.md](./RESOURCES.md)).
