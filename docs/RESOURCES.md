# Crisis Resources

**If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 911 (US) or your
local emergency number now.**

This list is a starting point, not exhaustive. Contributions that add,
correct, or verify a resource are always welcome — see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## United States

| Resource | How to reach it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline | Call or text **988** | Free, confidential, 24/7. Chat also available at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org). |
| Crisis Text Line | Text **HOME** to **741741** | Free, confidential, 24/7, US & Canada. |
| The Trevor Project | Call **1-866-488-7386**, text **START** to **678678**, or chat at [thetrevorproject.org](https://www.thetrevorproject.org) | For LGBTQ+ young people, 24/7. |
| Veterans Crisis Line | Call **988** then press **1**, or text **838255** | For veterans and their loved ones, 24/7. |
| SAMHSA National Helpline | Call **1-800-662-4357** | Substance use and mental health referral service, 24/7. |
| Trans Lifeline | Call **877-565-8860** | Peer support run by and for trans people. |

## International

If you can verify or add a country-specific line, please open a pull
request. Include an official source link for the number.

## Sourcing for statistics used on the site

- CDC, National Vital Statistics System / WISQARS — leading causes of death
  by age group: https://wisqars.cdc.gov/
- CDC Youth Risk Behavior Survey: https://www.cdc.gov/yrbs/
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: https://988lifeline.org
- Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, Means Matter project (survival
  rates after attempts): https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/means-matter/

Statistics change as new data is published. If you find a figure on this
site that is outdated or incorrect, please open an issue with the corrected,
sourced figure.
